Hanovh Social CSAE Compliance Terms

Updated: July 22, 2026

Hanovh Social is built to foster a safe federated space. This document describes the specific protections, responsibilities, and response procedures we follow to address and prevent any abuse related to child sexual exploitation.

1. What We Prohibit

On Hanovh Social, any material or conduct that depicts, describes, or promotes the sexual abuse or exploitation of minors is strictly forbidden.

  • Images, videos, or audio content involving child sexual abuse are prohibited.
  • Sexual descriptions or fantasies involving persons under the age of 18 are not permitted.
  • Any attempt to trade, distribute, or possess such content through our service violates these terms.
  • Attempts to induce minors into sexual activity, exploitative behavior, or grooming are strictly banned.

2. Reporting and Removal

When violation reports are received, Hanovh Social acts quickly to remove offending material and block accounts or federated sources that serve CSAE content.

  • Users may report content through the platform reporting system, or by contacting our safety team directly.
  • Reports are reviewed by our moderation team without unnecessary delay.
  • We remove confirmed CSAE material immediately and retain records needed for legal obligations.
  • We may suspend or terminate accounts when they are linked to abuse or exploitation.

3. Cooperation with Authorities

Hanovh Social cooperates with law enforcement and appropriate agencies when there is evidence of child sexual abuse exploitation.

  • When required by law, we disclose information to investigate and prosecute criminal activity.
  • We may preserve records and content relevant to public safety investigations.
  • We keep disclosure limited to what is necessary, consistent with legal requirements.

4. Moderation Approach

We maintain a combination of automated safeguards and human review to prevent CSAE content from being shared or propagated.

  • Automated filters help identify harmful material early.
  • Human moderators verify reports and unusual activity linked to CSAE risk.
  • Federated servers that repeatedly distribute abusive content may be blocked across the network.

5. User Responsibilities

All Hanovh Social participants must avoid uploading, posting, or sharing any content that violates these terms.

  • Do not create or transmit any sexual content involving minors.
  • Do not engage in language or behaviour that encourages child exploitation.
  • Report abuse immediately if you encounter suspicious or harmful material.

6. Safe Federation

Because Hanovh Social connects to other federated instances, we apply the same standard of CSAE protection across those interactions.

  • We do not allow relaying of known abusive feeds from other servers.
  • We may sever connections to instances that fail to enforce child protection standards.
  • Our trust signals reflect safety practices, not just technical compatibility.

7. Escalation and Review

If an issue cannot be resolved immediately, we escalate it to our senior safety coordinator and review it under our public safety framework.

  • Cases involving potential CSAE are prioritized for review.
  • We may preserve evidence and share it with law enforcement while maintaining user privacy where appropriate.
  • We revise this page as regulations change to keep our policies current.

Need to report urgent abuse? Contact us at safety@han.ovh and, if appropriate, your local authorities.
