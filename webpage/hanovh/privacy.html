Hanovh Social Media Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Hanovh Social Media is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and the rights you have in relation to your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and personalize our services. Information may be collected when you register, post content, interact with other users, or use features of Hanovh Social Media.

  • Account information: name, username, email address, profile data, and password.
  • Content and activity: posts, messages, comments, likes, follows, profile updates, and other activity on the platform.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, page views, navigation paths, and usage patterns.
  • Communications: support requests, feedback, and other communications you send to us.
  • Federated server data: information received from or shared with other federated instances when interacting across the fediverse.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • provide and maintain the Hanovh Social Media service;
  • enable account creation, authentication, and security;
  • personalize your experience and recommend relevant content;
  • improve, optimize, and develop new features;
  • communicate with you about updates, policy changes, and support requests;
  • protect the rights, property, or safety of Hanovh and our users.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to store your preferences, authenticate sessions, and understand how you use our site.

  • Essential cookies help us keep the site secure and remember your login state.
  • Performance cookies allow us to analyze usage trends and improve service quality.
  • Preference cookies store language, display, and usability settings.

You can control cookie settings through your browser, but disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the service.

4. Third-Party Services

Hanovh Social Media may use third-party services to support hosting, analytics, infrastructure, and communications. These providers may process personal data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We only share data as described in this policy or with your explicit consent.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We may disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • to comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from public authorities;
  • to enforce our Terms of Service and protect the rights, property, or safety of Hanovh, our users, or others;
  • when we believe disclosure is necessary to prevent harm or secure the platform;
  • with service providers who perform services on our behalf, such as hosting, storage, or security;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, where the receiving party agrees to use your information consistently with this policy.

6. User Rights

Your rights may include the following, subject to applicable law:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of your account and personal data;
  • object to our processing or request restrictions in certain cases;
  • opt out of marketing communications when applicable.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

8. Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we strive to protect your data in accordance with industry best practices.

9. Children

Hanovh Social Media is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

Hanovh Social Media may process and store information in servers located in different countries. By using the service, you agree that your information may be transferred to and processed in these locations in accordance with this policy.
